# AI Systems

NPC AI systems - behavior trees, pathfinding, perception, NPC management.

## Files

- `BehaviorTree.h/cpp` - Behavior tree framework
- `Pathfinding.h/cpp` - Pathfinding system (Recast/Detour)
- `PerceptionSystem.h/cpp` - AI perception (sight, hearing)
- `NPCManager.h/cpp` - NPC lifecycle management
- `AISquad.h/cpp` - Squad/group AI coordination

## Responsibilities

- NPC behavior logic
- Pathfinding and navigation
- Perception and awareness
- NPC spawning and management
- Squad coordination
