# World Management

City world management - city generation, arenas, streaming, portals.

## Files

- `CityManager.h/cpp` - City world management
- `ArenaManager.h/cpp` - Shooting arena management
- `WorldStreamer.h/cpp` - Large world streaming
- `PortalSystem.h/cpp` - Portal/teleportation system
- `SpawnSystem.h/cpp` - Spawn point management

## Responsibilities

- Generate and manage city layout
- Load/unload world chunks
- Manage shooting arenas
- Handle teleportation between zones
- Manage spawn points
